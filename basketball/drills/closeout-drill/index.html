Closeout Drill

Most open threes come from bad closeouts. In this drill, the defender starts under the rim with the ball, passes to a shooter on the wing, and closes out: sprint most of the way, then chop the feet with a hand up. The shooter can shoot or drive one dribble, so the defender has to contest without flying by.

How the drill works

  1. The defender starts under the rim with the ball and passes to the shooter on the wing.
  2. Closeout: sprint the first two-thirds, then chop the feet the last few steps, hand high, weight back.
  3. The shooter drives baseline. The defender slides and cuts off the drive without reaching.

Coaching points

  • Sprint, then chop: short, quick steps as you arrive.
  • High hand contests the shot; the other hand covers the drive.
  • Arrive with your weight back so you can slide either way.
  • Take away the middle or baseline, whichever your team rule says.

Variations

  • Make it live 1-on-1 with two dribbles.
  • Closeout from the help side of the lane.
  • Add a second shooter: defender closes out to whoever gets the ball.
