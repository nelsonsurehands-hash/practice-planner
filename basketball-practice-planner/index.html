      Questions coaches ask

      Is the basketball practice planner free?

      Yes. You can build a practice on this page without an account, and creating a team in The Extra Coach to save your plans is free.

      Can I print my practice plan?

      Yes. Print it here, or in the app save it as a PDF with the drill descriptions and coaching points, ready to hand to your assistant coaches.

      Can I use it on my phone during practice?

      Yes. In the app, Run practice opens the plan full screen with a countdown for each drill, so you can leave the clipboard in the bag.

      Do my assistant coaches see the plan?

      Yes. Invite your staff and everyone sees the same plan, and anyone you let edit it can change it.
