Privacy Policy

The Extra Coach is an app for coaching staffs to plan practices, draw plays, run the season calendar and share it with players and families. This page explains what we collect, why, who helps us run the app, and how to get your information deleted.

The short version

We collect what the app needs to work: your account, and the team information you and your staff put in.

We don't sell your information, and we don't show ads in the app.

Coaches control their team's information. You can change or delete it any time, and you can delete your whole account in Settings.

Questions or requests: email coach@theextracoach.com.

What we collect

Your account. Your name and email address, and a password if you sign up with one. Passwords are stored encrypted by our database provider; we can't see them. If you sign in with Google or Facebook, we receive your name and email from them.

Team information you enter. Team names, logos and colors; practice plans, drills and plays; the season calendar; game results, stats, gameplans and scouting notes; and rosters. A roster can include players' names and numbers and, if a coach adds them, parent or guardian names, phone numbers and email addresses.

Information families enter. When a parent uses a game-day food sign-up link, they can enter their name and what they're bringing. Parents don't need an account.

Photos you choose to import. When you use “Add from a photo,” the photo is sent to our AI provider to read it (for example, a roster or a schedule). We keep the results you choose to save, plus a count of how many photos your team has imported. We don't keep the photo.

How the app is used. Which days you open the app, how many times, how long each visit lasts, which features are used, and how you found us (for example, a link from another coach). We use this to improve the app. It is tied to your account and visible only to the app's owner.

Homepage visits. We count, anonymously, how many people visit our homepage, how far down they scroll and whether they start signing up, grouped by the link they came from (for example, our Facebook ad). We don't record names, email addresses or IP addresses for this, and we don't use cookies to do it.

Information stored on your device. The app saves some settings on your phone or computer (for example, the last team you opened and your court look) so it opens quickly. You can clear these from your browser.

What we don't collect

We don't ask for birthdates, home addresses, grades, health or medical information, or payment information, and we ask coaches not to enter them.

We don't use advertising trackers in the app, and we don't sell or rent anyone's information.

How we use it

To run the app: save your team's work, share it with the staff you invite, and show families the calendar or sign-up pages you send them.

To keep the app secure and working, and to fix problems.

To understand which features coaches use so we can make the app better.

To email you about your account (for example, a password reset). We won't send you marketing email unless you ask for it, and every marketing email will have an unsubscribe link.

Who can see your team's information

Your staff. Coaches you invite to your team can see it, and edit it if the head coach allows. View-only members can see but not change it.

People you send links to. Some pages are made to be shared: the parent calendar, play links for players, the food sign-up page, and a play you send to another coach. Anyone with one of those links can view that page, so share them the way you'd share a team group text. A coach can turn a link off or change it at any time.

Nobody else. Other coaches using the app can't see your team.

Companies that help us run the app

Supabase stores the database and handles sign-in.

GitHub Pages and Cloudflare host and deliver the website and app.

Anthropic reads photos you choose to import (AI). Per its commercial terms, information sent through its API isn't used to train its models.

Google and Facebook, only if you choose to sign in with them.

They handle information only to provide their service to us. We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety.

Student and minor information

The Extra Coach is made for adults: coaches and staff. Students don't create accounts.

When a coach enters a roster, the coach (and their school or organization) decides what goes in it and stays in control of it. We use roster information only to run the app for that team, and we delete it when the coach deletes it, deletes the team, or asks us to.

If you're a parent and want information about your child removed, ask your child's coach, or email us at coach@theextracoach.com and we'll help.

Keeping it safe

Each team's information is locked so only that team's staff can read or change it. Connections to the app are encrypted. We limit who can access the system and protect our own accounts with two-step sign-in.

No system is perfect. If we ever learn of a breach that affects your information, we'll tell you as the law requires.

Keeping and deleting information

We keep your account and team information while you use the app.

Delete your account: Settings → Your account → Delete my account. Teams that only you coach are deleted with everything in them. Teams with other staff stay with them. Usage history tied to your account is deleted too.

Or email coach@theextracoach.com and we'll do it for you. Backup copies are cleared on a rolling basis.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll update the date at the top and let coaches know in the app.

Contact

Email coach@theextracoach.com with any question or request about your information.
